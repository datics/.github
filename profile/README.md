# Datics AI

We bring production AI into the product you already run.

Datics is an AI product engineering company for established vertical B2B SaaS and EdTech companies, based in Austin, Texas. We call our work Brownfield AI: shipping AI into mature, integrated, permission-heavy software that already has paying customers, workflows and data, without a rebuild.

**How we think about it**

- AI belongs inside the product, not beside it. Our agent harness gives AI product context, trusted tools, permissions, tenant boundaries, memory, human approvals, audit trails and failure handling.
- Production constraints, not model quality, stop most AI features. We evaluate on real cases before anything ships.
- Change only what the capability needs. Every engagement has a name, a duration and a defined end state.
